## Full description (4000 chars max)
Play Whot! — the Nigerian card game — online with friends, or against bots.

No signup, no downloads of anything else: create a table, share the link,
deal. Your friends join from their phones. Anyone can watch the table live
in 3D.

ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
Create a table and share its link or code. Players join in seconds — no
accounts, no emails, no waiting. When someone is out, they're out. Last
player holding cards loses.

WATCH IN 3D
Send spectators a watch link and they follow the table in real time on a 3D
table — cards flying, turns animating. Great on a laptop or TV.

PLAY SOLO, OFFLINE
Bots with real Whot sense are built in. Practice without data, on the bus,
anywhere. The solo game ships inside the app and works fully offline.

REAL WHOT RULES
- Nigerian 5+1 dealing
- Star Whot and all special cards: pick two, pick three, hold on, general
  market, suspension
- Ask-for-suit on Whot cards
- Last to go out wins

NO SIGNUP, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
The app collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics. Just the game.

Whot! is the classic Nigerian card game played at every gathering. Now
play it with your people, wherever they are.
