# Privacy Policy — Last Card

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

Last Card ("the app") is a card game. This policy explains what data the app
handles — which is very little.

## Data the app does NOT collect
The app has no accounts and no sign-up of its own. It does not collect, store,
or share your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal information. It
contains no ads and no analytics/tracking SDKs.

## Game data stored on your device
Your game preferences and settings are stored locally on your device only.
They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Online play
Online tables are identified by a random room code. No personal information is
required to join a table. Gameplay within a table is visible to the other
players in that table and is deleted with the table.

## Google Play Games (scores)
If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your win score is submitted
to the Play Games leaderboard service operated by Google. That sign-in and the
data Google handles are governed by Google's own Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). Signing in is optional: the game works
without it.

## Android TV app
The TV app sends your gameplay to the online table you connect it to, so it
can display the live game. It collects nothing.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
any personal data from anyone, including children.
