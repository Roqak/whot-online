Last Card

Privacy Policy

Updated 29 September 2026 · lastcard.fun

Last Card is a card game. This policy explains what data the app handles — which is very little.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-up of its own. It does not collect, store, or share your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal information. It contains no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Game data on your device

Your game preferences and settings are stored locally on your device only. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Online play

Online tables are identified by a random room code. No personal information is required to join a table. Gameplay within a table is visible to the other players in that table and is deleted with the table.

Google Play Games (scores)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your win score is submitted to the Play Games leaderboard service operated by Google. That sign-in and the data Google handles are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Signing in is optional: the game works without it.

Android TV app

The TV app sends your gameplay to the online table you connect it to, so it can display the live game. It collects nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with an updated date.
